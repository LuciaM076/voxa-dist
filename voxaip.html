VoxaIP

Cloud telephony that scales with your business

VoxaIP is a hosted VoIP platform built for South African SMEs and call centres. Replace legacy PBX hardware with a flexible cloud phone system — desk phones, softphones, and mobile apps on one unified platform.

Key features

  • Virtual PBX with hunt groups, queues, and IVR menus
  • Local and geographic DIDs across major SA metros
  • Call recording, voicemail-to-email, and real-time analytics
  • SIP trunking and Microsoft Teams / CRM click-to-dial integrations
  • 99.9% uptime SLA with redundant data-centre routing

Works with Titan_CRM

Every inbound and outbound call can auto-log to the matching CRM contact. Agents get screen-pop with full history before they answer.

  • Automatic call disposition and duration sync
  • Queue metrics on your sales dashboard
